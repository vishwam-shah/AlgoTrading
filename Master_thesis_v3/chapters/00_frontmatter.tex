    % Abstract
    \chapter*{Abstract}
    \addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
    \pagestyle{fancy}
    This thesis presents a production-grade positional trading system for the Indian equity market, evaluated on the \textbf{Nifty-100 universe} (Nifty-50 + Nifty Next-50). For each stock, a heterogeneous \textbf{five-model ensemble} of classifiers (LightGBM, XGBoost, BiLSTM, TCN-Transformer, N-BEATS) is trained under a zero-leakage \textbf{expanding-window walk-forward protocol} with six chronological folds; a logistic-regression meta-learner blends the member probabilities and a single-parameter \textbf{temperature scaling} step calibrates the final output so that the gate threshold has an empirically meaningful interpretation.

    The contribution is fourfold: (i) a leakage-audited feature pipeline combining \(\sim\)200 OHLCV-derived features with global macro cues and an NSE-specific calendar, joined by strict prior-day temporal merge; (ii) a sector-aware force-include policy (USD/INR and Nasdaq pinned for IT stocks, RBI/Crude/DXY for banking); (iii) calibrated ensembling via validation-fitted temperature scaling on top of a logistic meta-stack; and (iv) a complete live deployment layer comprising an Angel One SmartAPI client, a paper trader with full Indian-market cost modelling (STT, SEBI, GST, exchange charges, slippage), an automated \textbf{promotion gate} that blocks live capital until pre-registered KPIs are met, and a FastAPI/React dashboard for human-in-the-loop oversight.

    Across all $100$ tickers the system delivers a clear edge on the deployable, high-confidence subset and, more importantly, translates that edge into a deployable portfolio: a top-15 cross-sectionally Sharpe-ranked equal-weight basket, trading the calibrated gated signal over a $526$-trading-day out-of-sample window (5 December 2023 -- 30 April 2026), yields a \textbf{total return of $\mathbf{170.65\%}$ against a Nifty-50 buy-and-hold of $\sim$$18\%$ over the same window}, with annualised Sharpe $1.18$, maximum drawdown $14.59\%$, and \emph{positive returns in every market regime} (bull, sideways, and bear) -- a headline result, transaction-cost-inclusive and regime-robust, that to our knowledge has not been reported on the Nifty-100 universe under a fully leakage-audited expanding-window walk-forward protocol. Full aggregate accuracies, the gated bootstrap confidence interval, the cross-sectional top-15 portfolio backtest, regime-conditional diagnostics, Diebold--Mariano significance tests, and per-ticker tables are reported in Chapter~\ref{chapter4}. Every run is fully reproducible: an immutable configuration record (random seed, train ratios, gating thresholds, feature flags) is written at run-start; per-window checkpoints, the per-ticker summary, fold details, the backtest portfolio, diagnostics, and next-day predictions are all persisted as structured artefacts that ship with the accompanying source repository.
