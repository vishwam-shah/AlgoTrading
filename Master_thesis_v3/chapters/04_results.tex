\chapter{Empirical Results} \label{chapter4}

All quantitative claims in this chapter are derived from two complementary artefacts. The \textbf{primary training run} (\(100\) tickers, six expanding-window folds per ticker, random seed~42, data from 1~January~2018, minimum predicted move \(\delta=0.004\), confidence threshold~0.58, train ratios \(0.70\!\to\!0.95\)) produced \(63{,}082\) out-of-sample predictions with a mean OOS accuracy of \(50.97\%\) and a mean F1-score of \(58.79\%\). The \textbf{thesis analysis report} (generated 8~May~2026) extends the same pipeline with a portfolio backtest, regime-conditional decomposition, Diebold--Mariano significance tests, and a paper-trading promotion-gate audit. All claims are reproducible against the committed source tree.

\section{The Gated Signal: Where the Edge Lies}

\subsection{Bootstrap Confidence Interval on Signal Days}

Once the signal gate is applied (calibrated probability \(\hat{p} \geq 0.58\) \emph{and} predicted absolute move \(\geq 0.4\%\)), a non-parametric bootstrap over the gated signal days yields:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bootstrap mean accuracy:} $0.5725$ ($57.25\%$)
    \item \textbf{Statistical significance against 50\% random:} \textbf{Yes} ($p < 0.05$); the bootstrap confidence interval strictly excludes $0.5$.
    \item \textbf{Margin over chance:} $+7.25$ percentage points.
\end{itemize}

This is the headline operational figure. On the days on which the system is willing to trade, its directional accuracy is significantly above chance, and the bootstrap confirms that this margin is not a Type-I artefact of multiple comparisons.

\subsection{Economic Significance of the Gate Threshold}

The threshold $\hat{p} \geq 0.58$ is not arbitrary: it corresponds to the empirically verified calibrated probability obtained by temperature scaling the ensemble meta-output against validation-slice observations. Relaxing the gate from $0.58$ to $0.55$ approximately doubles the signal count but reduces the bootstrap accuracy by 3--4 percentage points, illustrating the Goodhart-type trade-off between signal coverage and conditional accuracy.

\section{Probability Calibration Quality}

The fitted temperature $T^\star$ per fold per ticker is recorded in the per-fold summary. Roughly two-thirds of the fold--ticker cells have $T^\star \in [0.9,\ 1.1]$ (the meta-probability is approximately calibrated as-is); the remaining third has $T^\star \in [0.1,\ 0.9]$, indicating that the meta-probability was over-confident on the validation set and requires probability compression at test time. No cell in this run has $T^\star > 1.1$; the ensemble is structurally over-confident, never under-confident, which is the expected pattern from stacked ensembles~\cite{guo2017calibration}. Without the temperature step, the $0.58$ gate would over-trigger by roughly 20--30\% on a typical fold; the gate's operational meaning is contingent on the calibration step.

\section{Summary}

The results in this chapter establish four claims, each supported by verified artefacts from the run directory.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unconditional next-day direction is only marginally predictable.} All-days OOS accuracy across 100 tickers and 63{,}082 predictions is 50.97\%; this is consistent with weak-form market efficiency.
    \item \textbf{Conditional on the signal gate, the edge is real and statistically significant.} On the days where $\hat{p} \geq 0.58$ and $|\widehat{\Delta}| \geq 0.4\%$, the bootstrap accuracy is 57.25\%, statistically significant at $p<0.05$ with the confidence interval excluding 50\%.
    \item \textbf{The gated signal translates into a profitable, regime-robust portfolio.} Top-15 equal-weight cross-section returns 170.65\% over $\sim$2.5 years versus a Nifty-50 buy-and-hold of $\sim$18\%, with Sharpe 1.18 and max DD 14.59\%; the portfolio is profitable across all three market regimes.
    \item \textbf{The model significantly outperforms momentum and AR(1) baselines (DM $p<0.001$), but does not significantly beat always-up on the pooled test.} This is an honest and expected result for a positive-drift large-cap panel.
\end{enumerate}
