\chapter{Empirical Results} \label{chapter4}

All quantitative claims in this chapter are derived from two complementary artefacts. The \textbf{primary training run} (\(100\) symbols, six expanding-window folds per symbol, random seed~42, data from 1~January~2018, minimum predicted move \(\delta=0.004\), confidence threshold~0.58, train ratios \(0.70\!\to\!0.95\)) produced \(67{,}830\) out-of-sample predictions with a mean OOS accuracy of \(51.25\%\) and a mean F1-score of \(58.77\%\). The \textbf{thesis analysis report} (generated 8~May~2026) extends the same pipeline with a portfolio backtest, regime-conditional decomposition, Diebold--Mariano significance tests, and a paper-trading promotion-gate audit. All claims are reproducible against the committed source tree.

\section{The Gated Signal: Where the Edge Lies}

\subsection{Bootstrap Confidence Interval on Signal Days}

Once the signal gate is applied (calibrated probability \(\hat{p} \geq 0.58\) \emph{and} predicted absolute move \(\geq 0.4\%\)), the model generates \(1{,}495\) signal-days in the canonical run. A non-parametric bootstrap with \(10{,}000\) resamples over these signal days yields:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Bootstrap mean accuracy:} $0.6033$
    \item \textbf{95\% bootstrap CI:} $[0.5799,\ 0.6281]$
    \item \textbf{Significance against 50\% random:} \textbf{Yes}. The lower CI bound at $57.99\%$ strictly excludes $0.5$.
\end{itemize}

This is the headline operational figure. On the days on which V3 is willing to trade, the system is right in approximately three out of five instances, and the bootstrap confirms that this margin is not a Type-I artefact of multiple comparisons.

\subsection{Economic Significance of the Gate Threshold}

The threshold $\hat{p} \geq 0.58$ is not arbitrary: it corresponds to the empirically verified calibrated probability obtained by temperature scaling the ensemble meta-output against validation-slice observations. Relaxing the gate from $0.58$ to $0.55$ approximately doubles the signal count but reduces the bootstrap accuracy by 3--4 percentage points, illustrating the Goodhart-type trade-off between signal coverage and conditional accuracy.

\section{Probability Calibration Quality}

The fitted temperature $T^\star$ per fold per symbol is recorded in the per-fold summary. Roughly two-thirds of the fold--symbol cells have $T^\star \in [0.9,\ 1.1]$ (the meta-probability is approximately calibrated as-is); the remaining third has $T^\star \in [0.1,\ 0.9]$, indicating that the meta-probability was over-confident on the validation set and requires probability compression at test time. No cell in this run has $T^\star > 1.1$; the ensemble is structurally over-confident, never under-confident, which is the expected pattern from stacked ensembles~\cite{guo2017calibration}. Without the temperature step, the $0.58$ gate would over-trigger by roughly 20--30\% on a typical fold; the gate's operational meaning is contingent on the calibration step.

\section{Summary}

The results in this chapter establish four claims, each supported by verified artefacts from the run directory.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Unconditional next-day direction is only marginally predictable.} All-days OOS accuracy across 100 symbols and 67{,}830 predictions is 51.25\%; this is consistent with weak-form market efficiency.
    \item \textbf{Conditional on the signal gate, the edge is real and statistically significant.} On the 1{,}495 days where $\hat{p} \geq 0.58$ and $|\widehat{\Delta}| \geq 0.4\%$, the bootstrap accuracy is 60.33\% with a 95\% CI of $[57.99\%,\ 62.81\%]$, excluding 50\%.
    \item \textbf{The gated signal translates into a profitable, regime-robust portfolio.} Top-15 equal-weight cross-section returns 225.5\% over 2.5 years versus a Nifty-50 buy-and-hold of 18.7\%, with Sharpe 1.66 and max DD 21.5\%; the portfolio is profitable across all three market regimes.
    \item \textbf{The model significantly outperforms momentum and AR(1) baselines (DM $p<0.001$), but does not significantly beat always-up on the pooled test.} This is an honest and expected result for a positive-drift large-cap panel.
\end{enumerate}
